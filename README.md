# The World Chronicle — Wednesday 18 November 2026

A newspaper front page summing up the world's news of
Wednesday 18 November 2026.

## The stories

- **Anthropic prices its IPO at $83 a share.** The AI company
  priced above its $70–$78 range, for a fully diluted valuation
  of about $1.5 trillion, ahead of its Nasdaq debut on
  19 November under the ticker ANT.
- **Voyager 1 reaches one light-day from Earth**, about
  16.1 billion miles out, 49 years after launch.
- **A Van Gogh sells for a $190.4M auction record** —
  *Chestnut Trees* (1890) at Sotheby's New York.
- **The Gulf repels Iranian drones** — Bahrain and Kuwait
  intercept after U.S. strikes.
- **Kahana is elected Knesset Speaker, 66–52**; the
  Netanyahu bloc loses the vote.
- **A magnitude 5.3 quake hits Romania**, felt in
  17 nations, with no major damage.

## The program

`main.py` copies `entry.png` (the front page image in this
repository) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`, creating the
output directory if needed. It uses only the Python standard
library.
